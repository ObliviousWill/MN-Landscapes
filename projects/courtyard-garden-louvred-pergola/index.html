Courtyard garden with a louvred pergola

A small new-build plot turned into a courtyard: grey porcelain, dark metal planters, artificial lawn and a louvred pergola you can sit under whatever the weather.

Get a free quote Call 07387 085952

The brief

The garden a new house comes with — narrow, overlooked on both sides, and too small to waste any of it on something that does not earn its place.

What we did

  • Paved in large-format grey porcelain, run wall to wall so the space reads as one room rather than three strips
  • Built dark metal raised planters along both boundaries, holding the planting up where you can see it
  • Laid artificial grass in the middle — the right answer here, with this much shade from the neighbouring houses
  • Set a charcoal louvred pergola over the seating area, so it is usable in the rain and shaded in July
