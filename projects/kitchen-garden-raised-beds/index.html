Kitchen garden with raised beds

Large timber raised beds set among buff block-paved paths and strips of lawn, planted for vegetables, herbs and cutting — shown newly planted and a season on.

Get a free quote Call 07387 085952

The brief

A big, flat, open plot beside new buildings, wanted as a working kitchen garden rather than an ornamental one — and it had to be reachable with a barrow in February.

What we did

  • Built large timber raised beds at a comfortable working height, laid out so every bed can be reached from both sides
  • Ran buff block-paved paths between them, wide enough for a wheelbarrow and firm underfoot all year
  • Kept strips of lawn between the paving and the beds, so the garden does not read as a yard
  • Planted vegetables, herbs and perennials together, so the beds earn their keep and still look like something
