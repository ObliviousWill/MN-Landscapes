Cottage garden gin terrace

A new terrace in wood-effect porcelain planks, forming a walkway from the main lawn through to a large paved sitting area wrapped in deep planting beds.

Get a free quote Call 07387 085952
PhotoFinished terrace, evening

The brief

The owners had a long cottage garden with a good lawn and nowhere comfortable to sit at the far end of it — which was, of course, the end that caught the evening sun.

What we did

  • Set out a walkway in wood-effect porcelain planks, running from the main lawn down through the garden
  • Levelled and built a large paved sitting area at the end of the walk, sized for a table and six chairs
  • Cut deep planting beds around three sides so the terrace sits inside the garden rather than on top of it
  • Planted mixed cottage borders chosen for scent and for colour across the whole season

  • MaterialsWood-effect porcelain planks, Sawn stone edging, Levelled sub-base, Mixed cottage planting
