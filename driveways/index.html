The rule most people have not heard of

In England, if you are laying more than five square metres of impermeable surface in a front garden, you need either planning permission or somewhere for the water to go — a permeable surface, or a border, gravel margin or soakaway sized to take the run-off. It has been that way since 2008, and it is the thing that catches people out after the work is done.

We design the drainage in at quote stage, so the driveway is compliant without a retro-fitted channel drain across the middle of it.

The three surfaces worth considering

  • Block paving. The workhorse. Handles turning loads, easy to lift and relay, and permeable versions with open joints solve the drainage question outright.
  • Gravel. Inexpensive, naturally permeable and very much at home in front of a Norfolk cottage. Needs a proper edge and a stabilising grid if you do not want it in the road.
  • Resin-bound. Smooth, quiet and permeable, with no loose stone to track indoors. Unforgiving of a poor base, so it is only worth doing on one built for it.

Edges, levels and the bit at the road

Driveways fail at their edges first, so we haunch them in concrete. We set levels so water never runs towards the house or a neighbour, and where you need a wider crossing or a dropped kerb, that is a highways application to the county council — we will tell you if yours needs one before you commit.

Common questions

Do I need planning permission for a driveway?

Not if the surface is permeable, or if run-off drains to a border or soakaway within your own boundary. Over five square metres of impermeable surface with no drainage provision does need an application. We design to avoid the problem.

How long is a driveway off the road?

Usually one to two weeks. You will normally lose use of it for most of that, and we will agree with you where to park before we start.

Can you widen the crossing onto the road?

Altering a kerb or footway crossing is a highways matter for Norfolk County Council, not something a contractor can simply do. We will point you at the right application.

Which lasts longest?

A well-built block paved drive on a proper sub-base will outlast the rest, and it is the easiest to repair in patches if a service ever has to be dug up.
